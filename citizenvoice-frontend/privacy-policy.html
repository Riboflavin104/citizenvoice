Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email, and phone number. We use Aadhaar numbers solely for verification purposes and do not store the full number.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to process civic grievances, communicate updates regarding your complaints, and ensure secure authentication.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your data. Access to full complaint data is restricted to authorized municipal officials only.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preferences.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@citizenvoice.in.
